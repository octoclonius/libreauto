# LibreAuto

LibreAuto is a free and open-source alternative to Android Auto. It is designed to support apps installed from outside the Google Play Store and does not depend on Google Play Services.

## Requirements

1. Android 17+
2. [Shizuku](https://github.com/thedjchi/Shizuku)
